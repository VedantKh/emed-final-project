# Data Breach & Privacy Crisis: Decision Points & Follow-up Questions

## Decision Point 1: Disclosure Timing and Transparency

**Main Question:**
How would you approach the timing and extent of disclosure about the data breach to affected members, regulators, and the public?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. How do you balance the need for a complete investigation with the urgency of notifying affected individuals about potential identity theft risks?
2. What specific information would you include or withhold in your initial communications about the breach?
3. How would your disclosure approach differ for various stakeholders (affected members, all members, regulators, media)?

## Decision Point 2: Remediation and Member Protection

**Main Question:**
What specific remediation steps would you implement to protect affected members and rebuild trust in your company's data security?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. How would you determine the appropriate level of credit and identity monitoring services to offer affected members?
2. What factors would guide your decision about whether to establish a compensation fund for members who experience direct financial harm?
3. Beyond technical fixes, what organizational changes would you consider to demonstrate a serious commitment to preventing future breaches?

## Decision Point 3: Leadership Accountability

**Main Question:**
How would you address questions of leadership accountability for this breach, both internally and with external stakeholders?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. What criteria would you use to determine if personnel changes in your security or technology leadership are necessary?
2. How would you respond to board members or shareholders calling for executive accountability, potentially including your own position?
3. What approach would you take to balance acknowledging responsibility with protecting the company from legal liability?

# Pharmaceutical Pricing Crisis: Decision Points & Follow-up Questions

## Decision Point 1: Coverage Policy Development

**Main Question:**
What coverage policy would you establish for Vitacura, balancing patient needs, clinical evidence, and financial sustainability?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. How would you incorporate both clinical effectiveness data and cost considerations into your coverage decision-making process?
2. What specific coverage criteria or limitations might you consider to balance access with financial impact?
3. How would you approach the ethical dimension of potentially denying a life-extending treatment based on cost?

## Decision Point 2: Stakeholder Communication Strategy

**Main Question:**
How would you communicate your Vitacura coverage decision to members, providers, regulators, and the public?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. How would you explain your coverage decision to Sarah Chen and other patients directly affected by your policy?
2. What approach would you take with employer clients concerned about premium increases resulting from covering high-cost treatments?
3. How would you respond to media criticism that portrays your company as valuing profits over patients' lives?

## Decision Point 3: Long-term Strategy for High-Cost Treatments

**Main Question:**
Beyond this specific case, what long-term strategy would you develop to address the growing challenge of breakthrough but extremely expensive treatments?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. What innovative payment or risk-sharing models might you explore with pharmaceutical companies for high-cost treatments?
2. How would you engage with policymakers to address the systemic challenges of drug pricing while ensuring continued medical innovation?
3. What internal processes or structures would you establish to better prepare your organization for future high-cost treatment decisions?

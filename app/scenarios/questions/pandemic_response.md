# Pandemic Response Crisis: Decision Points & Follow-up Questions

## Decision Point 1: Telehealth Expansion Strategy

**Main Question:**
How would you address the massive surge in telehealth demand while ensuring quality care for members during this pandemic?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. How do you balance the immediate need for expanded telehealth access with concerns about quality control for providers who haven't previously offered virtual care?
2. What considerations would you make for vulnerable populations who may have limited technology access or digital literacy?
3. How would you weigh the short-term investment costs against long-term strategic benefits of telehealth infrastructure improvements?

## Decision Point 2: Financial Sustainability vs. Member Coverage

**Main Question:**
Given the projected $1.2 billion in COVID-related care costs and declining reserves, what approach would you take to balance financial stability with member coverage needs?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. What specific factors would guide your decision about whether to waive cost-sharing for COVID treatments as some competitors have done?
2. How would you explain your financial decision-making to members who are experiencing economic hardship due to the pandemic?
3. What metrics would you use to determine if your financial strategy is successfully balancing member needs with business sustainability?

## Decision Point 3: Public Relations and Stakeholder Communication

**Main Question:**
With BlueHorizon ranking in the bottom quartile of the "COVID Coverage Scorecard" and negative social media trending, what communication strategy would you implement to address stakeholder concerns?

**Follow-up Questions:**

1. How would you prioritize communications among different stakeholders (members, providers, regulators, investors) who may have conflicting interests?
2. What specific actions would you take to rebuild trust with members who feel the company is putting profits over their healthcare needs?
3. How would you balance transparency about business challenges with the need to maintain public confidence in your company's stability?

# Pharmaceutical Pricing Crisis: Evaluation Rubric

## Ethical Dimension (1-10)

### 1-3: Decisions that prioritize company interests at significant expense to stakeholders

- Focuses primarily on financial metrics and shareholder returns
- Denies coverage for life-extending treatments based solely on cost
- Shows minimal empathy for affected patients and their families
- Lacks transparency about coverage decision-making process
- Makes decisions that prioritize short-term profits over member health outcomes

### 4-6: Decisions that balance company and stakeholder interests with some compromises

- Attempts to balance financial sustainability with patient needs
- Implements limited coverage with significant restrictions or high cost-sharing
- Shows moderate empathy and concern for affected patients
- Provides some transparency about coverage decision-making process
- Makes decisions that consider both business viability and member health outcomes

### 7-10: Decisions that demonstrate exceptional ethical leadership, transparency, and stakeholder care

- Prioritizes patient access to life-extending treatments while seeking sustainable solutions
- Implements innovative coverage approaches that maximize patient access
- Shows deep empathy and proactive support for affected patients and families
- Provides full transparency about coverage decision-making process
- Makes decisions that place appropriate value on human life while acknowledging financial realities

## Strategic Dimension (1-10)

### 1-3: Short-term thinking with potential long-term negative consequences

- Focuses exclusively on immediate cost avoidance
- Makes reactive decisions without considering long-term implications
- Fails to engage constructively with pharmaceutical manufacturers
- Damages relationships with providers, patients, and advocacy groups
- Creates significant regulatory and reputational risks

### 4-6: Balanced approach with reasonable risk management and business continuity

- Balances immediate cost concerns with longer-term considerations
- Develops measured approaches to high-cost treatment coverage
- Engages in standard negotiations with pharmaceutical manufacturers
- Maintains adequate relationships with key stakeholders
- Manages regulatory compliance and reputational risks appropriately

### 7-10: Forward-thinking solutions that address immediate concerns while positioning for future advantage

- Develops innovative approaches to pharmaceutical pricing challenges
- Creates industry-leading frameworks for evaluating treatment value
- Builds collaborative partnerships with pharmaceutical manufacturers
- Strengthens relationships with providers, patients, and advocacy groups
- Positions the company as a thought leader in addressing healthcare affordability

## Specific Evaluation Criteria

### Coverage Policy Development

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Fairness and equity in treatment access
  - Transparency of clinical and financial criteria
  - Consideration of individual patient circumstances
  - Balance between population health and individual needs

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Financial impact management approach
  - Precedent-setting implications for future treatments
  - Competitive positioning relative to other insurers
  - Regulatory compliance and policy influence

### Stakeholder Communication Strategy

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Honesty and empathy in patient communications
  - Transparency about decision-making process
  - Respect for diverse stakeholder perspectives
  - Consistency between public statements and actual policies

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Media and public relations approach
  - Provider relationship management
  - Employer client communication strategy
  - Advocacy group engagement

### Long-term Strategy for High-Cost Treatments

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Commitment to expanding treatment access
  - Fairness in distributing healthcare costs
  - Transparency about long-term coverage philosophy
  - Balance between innovation support and affordability

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Pharmaceutical industry partnership approach
  - Policy advocacy strategy
  - Business model innovation
  - Long-term market positioning

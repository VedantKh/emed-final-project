# Data Breach & Privacy Crisis: Evaluation Rubric

## Ethical Dimension (1-10)

### 1-3: Decisions that prioritize company interests at significant expense to stakeholders

- Conceals or minimizes the breach to protect company reputation
- Delays notification to affected members beyond reasonable timeframes
- Provides minimal support for affected members experiencing identity theft
- Deflects responsibility and blames third parties without accepting accountability
- Makes decisions primarily to limit legal and financial exposure

### 4-6: Decisions that balance company and stakeholder interests with some compromises

- Provides timely but limited disclosure about the breach
- Offers standard credit monitoring services to affected members
- Accepts some responsibility while noting contributing external factors
- Balances transparency with legitimate legal considerations
- Makes reasonable efforts to support affected members

### 7-10: Decisions that demonstrate exceptional ethical leadership, transparency, and stakeholder care

- Provides prompt, comprehensive, and transparent disclosure about the breach
- Implements robust remediation services beyond standard credit monitoring
- Takes full accountability while implementing meaningful corrective actions
- Prioritizes member protection even at significant financial cost
- Demonstrates genuine empathy and commitment to making affected members whole

## Strategic Dimension (1-10)

### 1-3: Short-term thinking with potential long-term negative consequences

- Focuses primarily on immediate damage control and cost containment
- Fails to address underlying security vulnerabilities comprehensively
- Makes reactive decisions without considering long-term trust implications
- Damages relationships with regulators through minimal compliance
- Creates potential for greater reputational damage through inadequate response

### 4-6: Balanced approach with reasonable risk management and business continuity

- Implements necessary security improvements while managing costs
- Develops reasonable communication strategy that addresses key stakeholders
- Maintains adequate regulatory relationships through standard compliance
- Balances transparency with appropriate legal protections
- Takes steps to rebuild trust while managing financial impact

### 7-10: Forward-thinking solutions that address immediate concerns while positioning for future advantage

- Implements comprehensive security transformation beyond the immediate vulnerability
- Develops industry-leading data protection protocols and governance
- Builds stronger relationships with regulators through proactive engagement
- Turns crisis into opportunity by establishing the company as a privacy leader
- Creates long-term competitive advantage through enhanced member trust

## Specific Evaluation Criteria

### Disclosure Timing and Transparency

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Speed and completeness of notification to affected members
  - Clarity and honesty in communications about the breach
  - Transparency about the full scope and potential impacts
  - Balancing member right-to-know with investigation integrity

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Timing strategy for various stakeholder communications
  - Legal risk management approach
  - Media and public relations strategy
  - Regulatory compliance positioning

### Remediation and Member Protection

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Comprehensiveness of identity protection services offered
  - Financial support for members experiencing direct harm
  - Accessibility and ease of remediation services
  - Duration and quality of ongoing protection

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Cost-benefit analysis of remediation offerings
  - Competitive positioning relative to similar breaches
  - Long-term member retention strategy
  - Brand recovery approach

### Leadership Accountability

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Acceptance of responsibility at executive level
  - Transparency about causes and preventative measures
  - Fairness in addressing personnel accountability
  - Commitment to organizational culture change

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Governance and oversight improvements
  - Organizational structure changes
  - Investment in security infrastructure and talent
  - Long-term trust rebuilding strategy

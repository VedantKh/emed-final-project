# Pandemic Response Crisis: Evaluation Rubric

## Ethical Dimension (1-10)

### 1-3: Decisions that prioritize company interests at significant expense to stakeholders

- Focuses primarily on financial metrics and shareholder value
- Implements policies that create barriers to care during the pandemic
- Shows minimal transparency about coverage decisions
- Demonstrates little consideration for provider burnout or member hardship
- Makes decisions that could harm public health to preserve profits

### 4-6: Decisions that balance company and stakeholder interests with some compromises

- Attempts to balance financial stability with member needs
- Provides some expanded coverage for COVID-related care
- Shows moderate transparency in communications
- Acknowledges provider challenges and member hardships
- Makes decisions that generally support public health while maintaining business viability

### 7-10: Decisions that demonstrate exceptional ethical leadership, transparency, and stakeholder care

- Prioritizes member care access and provider support during the crisis
- Implements generous coverage policies for COVID-related care
- Demonstrates full transparency about decision-making processes
- Shows deep empathy and concrete support for struggling members and providers
- Makes decisions that actively contribute to public health even at short-term financial cost

## Strategic Dimension (1-10)

### 1-3: Short-term thinking with potential long-term negative consequences

- Focuses exclusively on immediate cost containment
- Makes reactive decisions without considering long-term implications
- Fails to invest in necessary infrastructure improvements
- Damages relationships with key stakeholders
- Creates potential regulatory and reputational risks

### 4-6: Balanced approach with reasonable risk management and business continuity

- Balances immediate needs with some longer-term planning
- Makes measured investments in necessary infrastructure
- Maintains adequate relationships with key stakeholders
- Manages regulatory compliance adequately
- Takes steps to protect company reputation

### 7-10: Forward-thinking solutions that address immediate concerns while positioning for future advantage

- Demonstrates innovative approaches to crisis management
- Makes strategic investments in telehealth and digital infrastructure
- Strengthens relationships with providers and members
- Anticipates regulatory changes and positions proactively
- Enhances company reputation through exemplary crisis leadership

## Specific Evaluation Criteria

### Telehealth Expansion Strategy

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Equity of access across diverse member populations
  - Quality of care standards for virtual visits
  - Support for providers transitioning to telehealth
  - Accommodations for vulnerable populations

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Infrastructure investment approach
  - Provider network expansion strategy
  - Long-term telehealth integration vision
  - Competitive positioning in virtual care

### Financial Sustainability vs. Member Coverage

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Fairness of cost-sharing policies
  - Support for financially vulnerable members
  - Transparency about coverage decisions
  - Balance between member needs and business sustainability

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Financial risk management approach
  - Regulatory compliance strategy
  - Competitive positioning relative to industry peers
  - Long-term business model sustainability

### Public Relations and Stakeholder Communication

- **Ethical considerations:**

  - Honesty and transparency in communications
  - Responsiveness to stakeholder concerns
  - Consistency between public statements and internal policies
  - Accountability for company decisions

- **Strategic considerations:**
  - Brand and reputation management
  - Stakeholder relationship management
  - Media and social media strategy
  - Crisis communication effectiveness
